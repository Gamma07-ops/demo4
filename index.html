About GreenScape

Our Story

Founded in 2010, GreenScape Landscaping has been transforming outdoor spaces across the community for over a decade. What started as a small family business has grown into a trusted name in professional landscaping.

Our team of certified horticulturists, landscape designers, and skilled technicians work together to bring your vision to life with creativity and precision.

Our Mission

We believe that beautiful outdoor spaces enrich lives and communities. Our mission is to design, create, and maintain landscapes that not only look stunning but also promote environmental sustainability.

We use eco-friendly practices, native plants, and water-conservation techniques to ensure our landscapes are as healthy for the planet as they are beautiful.

Our Services

Landscape Design

Custom landscape design tailored to your property's unique characteristics and your personal style.

Lawn Maintenance

Regular mowing, fertilizing, and weed control to keep your lawn healthy and vibrant all year round.

Irrigation Systems

Design and installation of efficient irrigation systems that save water and keep your landscape lush.

Hardscaping

Patios, walkways, retaining walls, and other structural elements to enhance your outdoor living space.

Seasonal Cleanup

Spring and fall cleanup services to prepare your landscape for the changing seasons.

Tree & Shrub Care

Pruning, disease treatment, and overall care for your trees and shrubs to ensure their health and beauty.
